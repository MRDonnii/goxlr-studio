## GoXLR Studio 0.5.9

**Sliders:** Afspilnings-EQ'en har nu tre visninger under kurven:
- **Tabel** – som før.
- **Sliders** – hvert bånd er en slider med til/fra, gain-værdi (slider og talfelt), frekvens, Q (og hældning for low/high cut), filtertype og øre. Yderst til venstre sidder en **preamp-slider**. "Lav sliders" laver 10, 15 eller 31 flade bånd.
- **Grafisk kurve** – den præcise GraphicEQ-kurve (fx fra AutoEQ) med en slider og et talfelt pr. bånd.

Alle tre viser de samme bånd, så du kan skifte frit. Ctrl+Z fortryder.

**Balance – hæv aldrig uden at sænke gulvet** (knappen "Balance", slået til fra start): når du løfter et bånd, sænkes preamp – hele resten af lyden – lige så meget. Så får du balance i stedet for bare mere lyd. Tager du løftet ned igen, kommer gulvet op igen. Du kan selv sætte preamp lavere, men ikke højere end balancen.

**Det skal du gøre:** Ingenting.

---

### English

**Sliders:** the Playback EQ now has three views below the curve:
- **Table** – as before.
- **Sliders** – each band is a slider with on/off, gain value (slider and number field), frequency, Q (and slope for low/high cut), filter type and ear. On the far left is a **preamp slider**. "Make sliders" makes 10, 15 or 31 flat bands.
- **Graphic curve** – the exact GraphicEQ curve (e.g. from AutoEQ) with a slider and a number field per band.

All three show the same bands, so you can switch freely. Ctrl+Z undoes.

**Balance – never boost without lowering the floor** ("Balance", on from the start): when you raise a band, the preamp – all the rest of the sound – goes down as much. That way you get balance instead of just more level. Take the boost down again, and the floor comes back up. You can set the preamp lower yourself, but not higher than the balance.

**What you need to do:** Nothing.

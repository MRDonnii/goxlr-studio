## GoXLR Studio 0.6.2

Små rettelser efter en gennemgang af hele appen:
- Diagnose-siden er nu helt på engelsk, når appen kører på engelsk (lydtjek-fund, genveje og apps).
- "Kopiér link" og "Kopiér token" siger nu fra, hvis Windows ikke tillader at kopiere, i stedet for at fejle stille.
- Esc under "Tilføj pedal" annullerer nu i stedet for at blive lært som pedal.

**Det skal du gøre:** Ingenting.

---

### English

Small fixes after a review of the whole app:
- The Diagnostics page is now fully in English when the app runs in English (sound check findings, hotkeys and apps).
- "Copy link" and "Copy token" now say so if Windows doesn't allow copying, instead of failing silently.
- Esc during "Add pedal" now cancels instead of being learned as a pedal.

**What you need to do:** Nothing.

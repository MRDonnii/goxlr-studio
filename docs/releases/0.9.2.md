## GoXLR Studio 0.9.2

- **Styr dine EQ-presets:** på EQ-siden under Presets er der nu en liste "Mine presets", hvor du kan **omdøbe** (✎), **kopiere** (⧉) og **slette** (✕) hvert preset – eller markere flere og slette dem på én gang. Omdøber du et preset, følger alt med: GoXLR-profiler, EQ pr. kanal, EQ pr. app, genveje, fodpedaler, enhedsautomatik og tidsstyring. Før du sletter, får du at vide, hvor presettet bruges. Indbyggede presets kan kopieres, så du får dit eget at rette i.
- **Import af Peace/Equalizer APO virker igen:** Peace gemmer sine presets i Windows' gamle tegnsæt (ANSI), og en enhed som "Højttalere" fik GoXLR Studio til at springe filen over uden at sige noget. Nu læses ANSI, UTF-8 og UTF-16. Knappen **"Hent Peace/APO-presets"** er der altid (før forsvandt den, når Equalizer APO var afinstalleret) og finder presets i GoXLR Studios backups – også backuppen fra Overflytning. Du kan også vælge filerne eller hele mappen med **"Importér mappe…"**. Presets, der allerede findes, importeres ikke igen.

**Det skal du gøre:** Ingenting. Mangler dine Peace-presets, så tryk "Hent Peace/APO-presets" på EQ-siden.

---

### English

- **Manage your EQ presets:** on the EQ page under Presets there is now a "My presets" list where you can **rename** (✎), **copy** (⧉) and **delete** (✕) each preset – or mark several and delete them at once. When you rename a preset, everything follows: GoXLR profiles, EQ per channel, EQ per app, shortcuts, foot pedals, device automation and the schedule. Before deleting you are told where the preset is used. Built-in presets can be copied, so you get your own to edit.
- **Importing from Peace/Equalizer APO works again:** Peace saves its presets in Windows' old character set (ANSI), and a device such as "Højttalere" made GoXLR Studio skip the file without a word. ANSI, UTF-8 and UTF-16 are read now. The **"Get Peace/APO presets"** button is always there (it used to disappear once Equalizer APO was uninstalled) and finds presets in GoXLR Studio's backups – including the one from Migration. You can also pick the files or the whole folder with **"Import folder…"**. Presets that are already there are not imported again.

**What you need to do:** Nothing. If your Peace presets are missing, press "Get Peace/APO presets" on the EQ page.

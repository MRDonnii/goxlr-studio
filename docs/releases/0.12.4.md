## GoXLR Studio 0.12.4

Rettelse: "Start med Windows" kunne stå som slået til, uden at Windows faktisk startede GoXLR Studio – fx efter en frisk Windows-installation.

- **Knappen viser nu det, Windows gør:** før viste "Start med Windows" bare den gemte indstilling. Var indstillingerne flyttet med "Flyt til ny pc", eller var appen installeret i en anden mappe, stod knappen til, selvom Windows ikke havde appen i sin autostart.
- **Genvejen skrives rigtigt:** GoXLR Studio skriver nu selv sin autostart med anførselstegn om stien (mappen "GoXLR Studio" har et mellemrum). Ved hver start tjekker appen, at genvejen findes og peger på den rigtige exe-fil, og den retter den, hvis ikke.
- **Slået fra i Windows:** har Windows slået GoXLR Studio fra under Indstillinger → Apps → Start, står det under knappen. Slår du "Start med Windows" til igen, tændes den også dér.
- **Hvor er appen?** Når GoXLR Studio er startet med Windows, ligger den i bakken ved uret. Windows 11 gemmer nye bakke-ikoner bag pilen ^. Første gang viser appen en kort besked om det.

**Det skal du gøre:** Start GoXLR Studio én gang i hånden efter opdateringen – så skriver den sin autostart-genvej. Tjek under Indstillinger, at "Start med Windows" er slået til.

---

### English

Fix: "Start with Windows" could show as on without Windows actually starting GoXLR Studio – e.g. after a fresh Windows installation.

- **The toggle now shows what Windows does:** before, "Start with Windows" only showed the saved setting. With settings moved by "Move to a new PC", or the app installed in another folder, the toggle was on although Windows did not have the app in its startup list.
- **The entry is written correctly:** GoXLR Studio now writes its own startup entry with quotes around the path (the "GoXLR Studio" folder has a space). At every start the app checks that the entry exists and points to the right exe, and fixes it if not.
- **Switched off in Windows:** if Windows has switched GoXLR Studio off under Settings → Apps → Startup, it says so under the toggle. Switching "Start with Windows" on again turns it on there too.
- **Where is the app?** Started with Windows, GoXLR Studio sits in the tray by the clock. Windows 11 hides new tray icons behind the ^ arrow. The first time, the app shows a short note about it.

**What you need to do:** Start GoXLR Studio by hand once after the update – it then writes its startup entry. Check under Settings that "Start with Windows" is on.

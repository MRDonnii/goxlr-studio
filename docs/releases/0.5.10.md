## GoXLR Studio 0.5.10

**Afspilnings-EQ:** visningen hedder nu bare **Sliders**, og knappen til at lave 10, 15 eller 31 flade bånd hedder **Lav sliders**.

**Det skal du gøre:** Ingenting.

---

### English

**Playback EQ:** the view is now simply called **Sliders**, and the button that makes 10, 15 or 31 flat bands is called **Make sliders**.

**What you need to do:** Nothing.
